# Renovate Kitchen

Explore a kitchen and dining room in 3D: compare finishes and furniture, move between room views, and try day and night lighting. Open doors and blinds, operate the hob controls, and experiment with the sink’s illustrative water flow.

**[Open the live demo →](https://peter-malcolm.github.io/renovate-kitchen/)**
